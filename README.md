# AI Music Gear – free trials

Free 14-day trials of AI Music Gear plugins and apps for Windows 10/11 (64-bit).
Every trial is the full product. After 14 days, activate it with the licence key
from your purchase.

**Product pages and purchases:** https://aimusicgear.com  
**Store:** https://aimusicgear.gumroad.com

## How to install
1. Open **Releases** (right side of this page) and find your product.
2. Under **Assets**, click the `…-Windows-Setup.exe` file to download it.
3. Run the installer. Windows may show a SmartScreen notice for new downloads:
   click **More info > Run anyway**. Installers are signed by AI MUSIC GEAR LLC.
